About Tanasa

Built by people who guide these routes themselves, not a booking desk that outsources them.

Tanasa Tours and Safaris is based in Zanzibar. Every itinerary here has been walked, sailed, or driven by someone on the team — from the alleys of Stone Town to the sandbanks off Nungwi and the plains beyond the channel. We keep groups small and routes flexible around tides, weather, and what you actually want to see.

Sample route

Four days, if this is your first time on the island.

We'll rebuild this around your dates, group size, and how much beach time you actually want.

Day 01

Arrival & Stone Town

Pickup from Abeid Amani Karume International Airport, check-in, and an afternoon walking tour through Stone Town's old quarter before dinner at the harbour-front night market.

Day 02

Spice farms & Kizimkazi

Morning spice tour inland, lunch of Zanzibari pilau, then south to Kizimkazi for a quiet coastal evening.

Day 03

Safari Blue sailing day

Full day out on a dhow to the sandbanks and mangrove channels, snorkeling stop, and a seafood lunch cooked on the sand.

Day 04

Nungwi & departure

Transfer north to Nungwi for a final morning on the beach, then an airport drop timed to your flight.
